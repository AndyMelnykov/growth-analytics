Recommendations

What to do with this

  1. Put real_time_collab in the first session. Prompt a co-edit or a teammate invite on day one. It is the biggest lever for both plans.
  2. Aim activation at the first week. Inactive users are the largest group. One or two hands-on actions lift day-14 conversion from 16.8% to 21.9%.
  3. Ask for the purchase while interest is high. A user on their 7th active free day who has not bought is settling in. That is when to show a plan prompt, introduce paid limits or have sales reach out.
  4. Lead Pro messaging with ai_insights, and Enterprise messaging with real_time_collab.
  5. After the purchase, push a second feature and the API. Fewer cancellations and more expansion. Test before treating these as effects.
Method and limits

How the numbers were made

Data

feature_usage_events (48,666), conversions (1,526), subscription_events (2,651), plus signup and release dates for timing. Calendar year 2024.

Time-aware comparison

Free days run from signup to purchase, up to 95 days. A behaviour counts from the day after it first happens. Rates are compared within days-since-signup band × signup month and pooled as a Mantel–Haenszel rate ratio with a 95% interval. A feature is only compared on days after its release.

Strict test

About 30 behaviours are tested per outcome. "Strict" means the interval still excludes 1.00× at z = 3.14 (Bonferroni for 30 tests).

Journey graph

Event sequences are built into a weighted directed graph with graph.build.build_graph and solved as an absorbing Markov chain. The removal effect is the drop in P(buy) from Signup when a feature node is cut.

Limits

The data is synthetic with engineered signals, so absolute rates are invented. These are associations, not proof of cause. The link between long free use and not buying may partly reflect how the generator spreads events.
